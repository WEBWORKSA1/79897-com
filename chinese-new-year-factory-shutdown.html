Chinese New Year 2027 factory shutdown playbook

Spring Festival 2027 begins on Saturday 6 February (official holiday 5–12 February). Counting the slowdown before and the restart after, production disruption typically runs 6–8 weeks.

Countdown to CNY 2027

The 2027 timeline (estimated)

WhenWhat happens
By late Nov 2026Recommended last purchase orders for sea freight delivery before the shutdown
Early–mid Dec 2026Latest realistic POs for air freight. Rates and space get tight.
Mid-Jan 2027Pre-holiday slowdown as migrant workers travel home
5–12 Feb 2027Official public holiday. Many factories are closed for 2–4 weeks.
20 Feb 2027Lantern Festival. Many factories restart around now.
Early–mid Mar 2027Capacity back to normal. 10–30% of workers may not return.

Based on lunar-calendar holiday dates and industry timelines. Official make-up working days are announced by the State Council, usually in November.

Week-by-week checklist

October–November 2026

  • Forecast demand through March 2027 and add a 4–6 week buffer.
  • Confirm each supplier's exact closing and reopening dates in writing.
  • Place POs and pay deposits. Lock raw-material prices.
  • Book freight space early, because pre-CNY rates tend to rise.

December 2026

  • Book pre-shipment inspections now, since inspectors get booked out in January.
  • Approve packaging and labels. Don't leave artwork changes for January.
  • Agree a written production schedule with milestones.

January 2027

  • Inspect and load before mid-January wherever you can.
  • Expect rushed work. Don't relax your AQL standard.
  • Pay balances only after you have the inspection report.

After the holiday

  • Inspect the first post-CNY batch closely, because new workers mean more defects.
  • Recheck prices, since some suppliers reset quotes after New Year.
  • Rebuild safety stock before the 618 season.

Work out your last safe order date

Last PO date = shipment-ready date − production lead time. The shipment-ready date should be about mid-January for sea freight. Add your transit time (sea is roughly 4–6 weeks door to door) to see when stock lands. Use the calendar to add all key dates to your own calendar.
