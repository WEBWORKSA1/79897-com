Three ways in

ModelHow it worksBest for
Cross-border e-commerce (CBEC)Sell from abroad via bonded warehouses or direct mail, with no Chinese entity. Many products avoid full domestic registration.Testing demand: beauty, supplements, baby, food, fashion
Distributor / importerA Chinese partner imports, registers and sells.Food and beverage, wine, B2B products
Local entity (WFOE)Your own Chinese company and domestic store.Brands with proven traction and budget

Most brands start with CBEC, use it to prove demand, then localise.

Platforms at a glance

  • Tmall Global: the premium cross-border marketplace. It has a strong trust halo, but expect deposits, annual fees and commission, and a TP (Tmall Partner) operator.
  • JD Worldwide: strong in electronics, appliances and authenticity-conscious shoppers, with its own logistics.
  • Douyin (TikTok's Chinese sibling): content-driven "interest e-commerce" built on livestreams and short video.
  • Xiaohongshu (RED): lifestyle discovery and reviews. It's essential for beauty, travel and fashion seeding.
  • WeChat: mini-program stores and private-domain community selling to repeat customers.

Content, KOLs and KOCs

Chinese shoppers research heavily. Seed reviews with KOCs (key opinion consumers: everyday users with small followings) on RED before you pay top-tier KOLs. Use livestreams for launches and festival peaks. Localise rather than translate: names, claims and visuals.

Plan around the festival calendar

The big moments are Chinese New Year (gifting), 520 and Qixi (romance), 618 (mid-year sale), Mid-Autumn (gifts), Double 11 and Double 12. Presales open 3–4 weeks before each peak, and stock must already be in a bonded warehouse. See the live China business calendar.

Cultural checklist

  • A Chinese brand name that sounds good and has positive characters. Check it with a native speaker.
  • Prices and bundle quantities that avoid 4 and favour 8, 6 and 9. Try the lucky price generator.
  • Red and gold for festive packaging. Avoid giving clocks, white flowers or green hats.
  • Launch dates checked against the lunar calendar and ghost month (the 7th lunar month).
