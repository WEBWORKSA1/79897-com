Incoterms & sourcing glossary

Every term a supplier or forwarder will throw at you, explained in one or two lines.

Incoterms 2020 (most used with China)

EXW (Ex Works)
You collect from the factory door and handle everything, including Chinese export clearance. Cheapest quote, most work for you.
FOB (Free On Board)
The supplier delivers goods onto the vessel at a named Chinese port (e.g. FOB Ningbo). The most common term. You control the main freight.
CIF (Cost, Insurance & Freight)
The supplier pays freight and minimum insurance to your port. Risk passes to you at loading. Destination charges are yours.
DAP (Delivered At Place)
Delivered to your address, but you pay import duty and taxes.
DDP (Delivered Duty Paid)
Delivered to your door with duties paid. The simplest for beginners. Check the price includes everything.

Sourcing & production

MOQ
Minimum order quantity per SKU or order. Often negotiable for a first trial order.
OEM
Original equipment manufacturing: the factory makes your design under your brand.
ODM
Original design manufacturing: the factory's existing design, customised and branded for you (private label).
Golden sample
The approved reference sample that both sides sign. Inspections compare against it.
Trading company
A company that sells products made by other factories. Useful for small or mixed orders.
NNN agreement
Non-use, non-disclosure, non-circumvention contract. It protects designs in China better than a Western NDA.

Quality

AQL
Acceptable Quality Limit: a sampling standard (ISO 2859-1) that sets how many defects are allowed in a random sample.
PSI
Pre-shipment inspection, done when at least 80% of goods are packed. The most important check.
DUPRO
During-production inspection, done at 20–60% completion to catch problems early.
Factory audit
An assessment of a factory's capability, systems or social compliance (e.g. BSCI, Sedex).

Shipping, customs & payment

HS code
Harmonized System code that classifies your product and sets the duty rate.
CBM
Cubic metres of shipment volume. L×W×H (cm) ÷ 1,000,000. Calculate it.
LCL / FCL
Less than container load (a shared container) vs full container load (20ft or 40ft to yourself).
Bill of lading (B/L)
The carrier's receipt and title document for sea cargo.
T/T
Telegraphic transfer (bank wire). Typical terms are 30% deposit and 70% before shipment.
L/C
Letter of credit: the bank pays the supplier when compliant documents are presented.
Landed cost
Total cost to get goods into your warehouse. Calculate it.
