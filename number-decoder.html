How the score works

Each digit carries a weight (8 and 4 carry the most, in opposite directions). Famous combinations add or subtract points: 168, 518, 888, 1314 and 98 add; 14, 74, 514 and 748 subtract. Palindromes and repeated lucky digits earn a bonus. The result is scaled 0–100.

Cultural guide, not fortune-telling. Meanings vary by dialect. In Cantonese, for example, 7 and 9 have slang uses. Treat the score as a branding signal, not a prediction.
