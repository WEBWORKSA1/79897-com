The formulas

  • CBM = L × W × H (cm) ÷ 1,000,000 × number of cartons.
  • Air volumetric weight = L × W × H ÷ 6,000 per carton. Express usually divides by 5,000.
  • Chargeable weight = whichever is greater, actual or volumetric.
  • Containers: a 20ft holds about 28 CBM usable and a 40ft about 58 CBM. Above about 15 CBM, a 20ft FCL often beats LCL.
