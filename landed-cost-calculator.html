Landed cost calculator

The factory price is only the start. Duty, tariffs, freight and fees often add 30–80%. Find your true cost per unit before you commit to a PO.

Rates differ by HS code and country, and tariffs change often. Check your official customs tariff database before you order.

How landed cost is calculated

Landed cost = goods + freight + insurance + duty + extra tariffs + import VAT/GST + broker, inspection, payment and other fees. Divide by quantity to get your cost per unit.

  • FOB vs CIF basis. The US, Canada and Australia generally charge duty on the transaction (FOB) value. The EU, UK and many others use CIF, which adds freight and insurance to the duty base.
  • Extra tariffs. Some products from China carry additional or trade-remedy duties (for example US Section 301 lists, or anti-dumping duties). These stack on top of normal duty.
  • VAT/GST is usually reclaimable for registered businesses, but it still costs you cash flow until you reclaim it.
  • Retail at target margin = landed cost ÷ (1 − margin). A 55% gross margin on a $7 landed cost needs about $15.56 retail.
