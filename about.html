起久发久起

Rise. Endure. Prosper.

79897 reads in Mandarin as rise, endure, prosper, endure, rise. We think that's the story of every good trading business, and our job is to make the "endure" part easier.

Our mission

Make trading with China clear, fair and profitable for small and mid-sized businesses. We do that with free calculators, an always-current business calendar, plain-English guides, and a matching service that connects buyers with vetted specialists.

Our method

  • Numbers first. Every guide links to a tool so you can test it on your own case.
  • Sources cited: customs data, official holiday schedules, published fee lists.
  • Clear labels: sponsored content and partner links are always disclosed.
  • Updated before every major season: CNY, 618, Double 11 and the Canton Fair.

How we make money

To keep everything free for buyers, 79897 earns from:

  1. Display advertising (Google AdSense), only with your consent.
  2. Partner referral fees from sourcing, QC, logistics and market-entry partners when they receive a qualified request.
  3. Sponsorships of tools, the calendar, the newsletter, videos and contests.
  4. Reader support through donations and patrons.

None of these change the price a partner quotes you. See the full disclosures.
