Countdown to Chinese New Year 2027 (Year of the Goat)

Factories slow from about mid-January and may be closed for 2–4 weeks. Place sea-freight orders by about late November 2026.

2027 holiday dates follow the lunar calendar and are expected dates. China's State Council usually confirms the official schedule, including make-up working days, in November. Canton Fair 2027 dates are expected. Check the official fair website.

How to use this calendar

  • Importers: work backwards from each holiday. Subtract production lead time, transit time and a 2–3 week buffer. That's your last safe PO date.
  • Brands selling to China: 618 and Double 11 presales start 3–4 weeks early. Campaign assets and stock at bonded warehouses must be ready before then.
  • Trade-fair visitors: book Guangzhou hotels early. Prices surge during Canton Fair phases.
