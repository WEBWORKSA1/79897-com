Frequently asked questions

General

Is 79897 free to use?

Yes. All tools, guides and the quote-matching service are free for buyers. We're funded by ads, sponsorships, partner referral fees and reader support.

What does 79897 mean?

Read in Mandarin, 七九八九七 suggests 起久发久起: rise, endure, prosper, endure, rise. It's also a palindrome. Full explanation.

Is this legal, tax or customs advice?

No. Everything here is general information. Confirm duty rates, regulations and contracts with licensed professionals.

Quotes & partners

How does the free quote service work?

You submit one request. We match it with up to three partners by category, order size and destination, and they reply to you directly, usually within 24–48 business hours.

How are partners vetted?

We check business registration, track record, references and responsiveness, and keep monitoring buyer feedback. Vetting reduces risk but doesn't remove it. Always run your own due diligence and inspections.

Do you sell my data?

No. Your request is shared only with up to three matched partners, for the purpose of quoting. See the Privacy Policy.

Tools

How accurate are the calculators?

The maths is exact, but the inputs (duty, freight rates, fees) are your estimates. Default freight rates are rough placeholders. Replace them with real quotes.

Are the calendar dates official?

2026 dates are confirmed. 2027 lunar holiday dates are the expected dates, pending State Council confirmation (usually in November). Factory shutdown windows are industry estimates.
