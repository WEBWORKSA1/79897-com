Benchmarks to aim for

  • Net margin of 20%+ after all fees and ads. Below that, a single bad batch or a price war erases profit.
  • Landed cost at or under 25–30% of retail for marketplace products (the "1/3 rule" or better).
  • First order cash at risk you could lose without hurting the business. Negotiate a smaller trial MOQ if needed.

Five ways to improve the numbers

  1. Ask for a tiered price: MOQ, 2× MOQ and 5× MOQ.
  2. Reduce packaging size to cut CBM and fulfilment tier.
  3. Compare 3+ factories. Agents typically find 10–25% spreads.
  4. Ship sea LCL instead of air once demand is proven.
  5. Test a price point ending in 8 for Chinese-speaking audiences. See the lucky price generator.
