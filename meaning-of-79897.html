The meaning of 79897 七九八九七

Read aloud in Mandarin as qī-jiǔ-bā-jiǔ-qī, 79897 suggests 起·久·发·久·起: rise, endure, prosper, endure, rise. It's a cycle of growth with lasting prosperity at its heart, and it reads the same in both directions.

Four features that make it distinctive

  1. A palindrome (回文数). 79897 reads the same forwards and backwards. Symmetry (对称) is central to Chinese aesthetics, from paired couplets (对联) to palace layouts.
  2. A prosperity core: 98 / 989. 久发 means "prosper for a long time", a phrase phone-number and plate sellers use as a selling point.
  3. No 4. The number avoids 四 (sì), which sounds like "death". Strictly, the digit sum is 40, which some sum-based readers reduce to 4. Most popular readings focus on the digits as spoken.
  4. It contains 798. This echoes Beijing's 798 Art Zone, a former factory complex turned arts district that recorded 12.57 million visits in 2024.
Honest caveats. 7 also echoes 欺 (deceive), and the 7th lunar month is "ghost month". In Cantonese slang, 7 and 9 have vulgar uses. The 起久发久起 reading is a popular homophone interpretation, not a classical idiom.

Is 79897 a lucky number?

Mostly positive in Mandarin: it holds two 9s (lasting), an 8 (prosperity), symmetry, and no 4. The 7s are mixed. It's best described as "auspicious with a growth story".
