Lucky price generator 吉祥定价

Western retail ends prices in 9. Chinese retail often ends them in 8, because 八 (bā) sounds like 发 (fā), "to prosper". Enter a price to get the closest auspicious alternatives, ranked.

Quick rules: end in 8 (prosperity) or 9 (longevity), use 6 for "smooth", never 4 (death). Combinations like 168 (一路发, "prosper all the way") and 888 carry premium appeal.

Where lucky pricing matters most

  • Gifting and festivals: red-envelope amounts (¥88, ¥168, ¥888), mooncake boxes, CNY hampers.
  • Weddings: 9 (久, lasting) and pairs (2) are prized. 1314 means "a whole lifetime".
  • Premium and luxury: 888 and 8888 signal abundance.
  • Real estate and car plates: buyers pay real premiums for 8s and discount 4s.
