Questions buyers ask

Is it really free?

Yes. Buyers never pay 79897. Partners may pay us a referral fee when they receive a qualified request. That's how we keep the tools free.

Who sees my request?

Only 79897 and up to three partners matched to your category. We don't publish requests or sell them in bulk. You can ask us to delete your data at any time.

What's the minimum order?

There's no minimum to ask. Many factories have MOQs from 300 to 1,000 units, but agents can often negotiate lower for a first order or find trading companies with stock.

Do you guarantee the suppliers?

No. We screen partners for track record and responsiveness, but every order needs its own due diligence. Always pay with protection and book a pre-shipment inspection. Our import guide explains how.

How fast will I hear back?

Usually within 24–48 business hours. Around Chinese holidays (see the calendar) it can take longer.
