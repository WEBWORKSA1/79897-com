CNY vs CNH vs RMB

RMB (renminbi) is the currency. Yuan is its unit. CNY is the onshore rate in mainland China, and CNH is the offshore rate traded in Hong Kong and abroad. Suppliers usually quote in USD for export or in CNY for domestic prices. Always confirm which one, and whether 13% VAT is included.

Paying Chinese suppliers

  • Bank wire (T/T): usually 30% deposit and 70% before shipment.
  • Trade-assurance or escrow on B2B platforms.
  • Letters of credit for large orders.
  • Never pay a personal account that differs from the company name on the contract.
