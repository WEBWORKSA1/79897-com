Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

What we collect

  • Form data you send us: name, email, company, phone or WhatsApp/WeChat, and request details.
  • Usage data, only with consent: pages viewed and device and browser information, via Google Analytics.
  • Advertising cookies, only with consent: Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can opt out of personalised ads at Google's Ads Settings or aboutads.info.
  • Local preferences: theme, cookie choice and popup status are stored in your browser's local storage, never on our servers.

How we use it

  • To reply to you and deliver what you asked for (quotes, newsletter, contest entries, applications).
  • To match quote requests with up to three relevant partners, only when you consent on the form.
  • To improve the site and measure performance.

Processors

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Forms are relayed by FormSubmit to our private inbox. Ads come from Google AdSense and analytics from Google Analytics 4 (both consent-based). Videos load from YouTube in privacy-enhanced (no-cookie) mode, only when you press play. Exchange rates come from ExchangeRate-API, and no personal data is sent.

Retention

Form submissions are kept for up to 24 months, or until you ask us to delete them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR, UK GDPR, PIPEDA, CCPA/CPRA or PIPL), you can ask to access, correct, delete or port your data, or object to processing. Use the contact form (topic: "Privacy / data request"). You can change cookie consent any time by clearing this site's storage in your browser.

Children

This site is for business users and isn't directed at children under 16.

Changes

We'll post updates here with a new date.
